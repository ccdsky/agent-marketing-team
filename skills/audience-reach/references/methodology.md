# Audience Reach — Extended Reference

## Lineage & Posture

Adapted from the `channel-discovery` framework (superamped/ai-marketing-skills), rebuilt persona-first.

- **Kept:** scoring outlets on fit; ruthless prioritization over "try everything."
- **Discarded:** the budget-first / runway framing (budget is a per-outlet output band here, not a gating input) and the "learning value" scoring criterion (startup/experiment-flavored; it doesn't serve a coordinated B2B reach motion).
- **Added:** persona-level outlet research and a buying-committee roll-up.

**Seam with `/market-research`:** its Audience Behavior Map is category-level and a competitive-intel byproduct. This skill is persona-level and names specific current outlets. They complement, they don't duplicate.

---

## Phase 3: RESEARCH — Medium-Type Checklists

For each persona, name **specific outlets, not categories**. A source/evidence note is required for each named outlet.

### Earned/Organic
- Publications & blogs the persona actually reads (check who their influencers cite)
- Podcasts that recur in their discussions
- Newsletters they subscribe to
- Communities/forums: named subreddits, Slack/Discord servers, LinkedIn/industry groups
- Social: specific groups, hashtags, creator accounts

### Paid
- Search: high-intent query themes worth bidding on
- Social ad targeting: the platform plus the specific audience definition that maps to this persona
- Programmatic / display contexts where the persona reads
- Named sponsorships: trade publications, podcasts, newsletters

### Events
- Specific conferences, trade shows, summits the persona attends
- Webinars / virtual events in their orbit
- Professional associations and local chapters

### Influence/Intermediary
- Industry analysts the persona trusts (seed from `icp.md`'s intermediary-influence map)
- Consultants / advisors / system integrators who shape their shortlist
- Creators / thought-leaders with real pull
- Partner / channel ecosystems that reach them

### Outbound
- Professional networks where direct outreach is welcomed
- Communities that permit vendor participation (note the norms)
- Where intent data or list sources are viable

## Phase 4: SCORE & ENRICH — Reach-Fit Guide

Score each outlet 1–5:

| Dimension | 1 | 5 |
|-----------|---|---|
| **Persona fit** | persona barely present | persona concentrated / native here |
| **Reachability** | hard or impossible to place a message | easy, accepted placement path |
| **Intent / signal quality** | passive awareness only | strong buying-intent signal |
| **Cost-to-access efficiency** | poor cost per qualified reach | excellent cost per qualified reach |
| **Effort / feasibility** | heavy build + ongoing upkeep, slow to activate | light, fast to activate |

**Cost-to-access band** (qualitative — never invent precise prices):
- **$** — low (organic participation, owned outreach)
- **$$** — moderate (newsletter/podcast placements, mid-tier events)
- **$$$** — high (major sponsorships, premium ad inventory, flagship trade shows)

**Intent read:** label each outlet **Awareness / Consideration / Decision** based on why the persona is there.

**Tie-breakers:** persona fit → intent quality → cost efficiency.

---

## Phase 5: ROLL UP — Coordination Method

- **Coverage matrix:** personas (rows) × outlets (columns); mark presence and fit.
- **Shared outlets:** reach 2+ committee members → efficiency plays.
- **Persona-unique outlets:** precision plays for a single decision-maker.
- **Timing/sequencing dependencies to flag** (flag, don't orchestrate — the Campaign Lead owns the calendar):
  - **Lead/lag:** which awareness motion should precede which conversion motion, and roughly by how long.
  - **Editorial-calendar anchors:** the content cadence an outlet expects (event-tied, seasonal).
  - **Market-timing anchors:** trade shows, fiscal/budget cycles, buying seasons, regulatory dates.

## Freshness & Confidence

Outlet maps are dated snapshots — named outlets change. Flag confidence per outlet:
- **HIGH** — corroborated by the dossier + 2+ current sources
- **MEDIUM** — single current source, or strong inference
- **LOW** — inference only; mark as "verify"

Never present a LOW-confidence outlet as a conclusion.
